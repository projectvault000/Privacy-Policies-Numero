Privacy policy for Numero

Numero is a numeracy learning app for students in grades 1 to 12, approximately ages 6 to 18. Operator: [CONFIRM OPERATOR NAME]. This policy explains local learner information, optional exports and the network services used by the app.

1. Learner profiles and local learning information

When a learner profile is created, Numero saves the learner's name, username, Learner Reference Number (LRN), school ID, grade and section on the device. A PIN protects access to the profile and is represented by a salted hash, not a stored plaintext PIN. The app saves practice, assessments, learning levels, progress and virtual rewards to support learning and identification of results by teachers. The current app does not create an online learner account or automatically upload learner profiles or results to an operator server.

2. Camera, images and QR codes

Camera permission is used to decode QR codes locally. Supported QR images and backup files can also be selected using the system file picker. The reviewed app does not upload camera frames or selected QR images for remote decoding.

3. Results and backups you choose to export

A results QR can contain the learner's name, identifiers, grade, section, progress and assessment results. Anyone able to scan or copy it may read the contents. Share it only with the intended teacher or recipient. Records received by a teacher or school are subject to that recipient's handling and retention.

A recovery backup is different from a results QR. Recovery files and QR images are encrypted and require the PIN or passphrase used to create them. You choose where exported files are saved through the system file picker. If you select a cloud storage provider, that provider may store the exported file under its own terms. Protect the backup and its credential.

4. Internet services and advertising

Numero downloads public school configuration and learning content from Google spreadsheets. The service receives connection metadata such as the device IP address. These requests are not automatic learner-profile or assessment-result uploads. Bundled or saved learning content works offline.

Numero integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on the enabled services and configuration, these providers process connection data, ad interactions, diagnostics and SDK identifiers for advertising, performance measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's privacy policy.

The current local implementation requests child-directed, under-age and general-audience advertising for all learner profiles, disables publisher first-party ID and does not request advertising-ID or AdServices permissions. Learner names, LRN, school details and results are not supplied to the ads SDK for targeting. Visual themes do not determine age eligibility. These settings do not mean that network services receive no information. Learning remains available when ads or consent services are unavailable. Where the consent service requires an options entry point, Profile offers Ad privacy choices.

5. Storage, security and retention

The learning database uses SQLCipher encryption. A random database passphrase is wrapped using an Android Keystore key. Saved teacher-test data also uses encrypted local storage. These measures do not protect against every threat, including an unlocked or compromised device.

Local learner data remains until removed from the device. The app does not impose an automatic expiration on saved learner profiles. Exported files, information already received by others and provider-held information have separate retention periods. 6. Deletion and your choices

In Profile, select Delete profile and enter the learner's PIN. This removes that learner's local profile and related progress, rewards and saved tests; other profiles remain. Local data can also be removed using system app-data controls or uninstalling the app.

Deletion does not erase independent backup files, QR images, records already received by a school or teacher, or information held by advertising/storage providers. A saved recovery backup may still restore its saved contents. Delete exported copies you control and contact their recipients or providers about copies they retain. This app does not present local removal as deletion of a server account.

You can decline camera permission, use supported file alternatives, learn offline, review applicable ad privacy choices and delete local profiles. Contact the operator for privacy questions.

7. Children and families

Numero serves school-age students, including children. The same protective advertising settings apply to both visual themes. No exact birth date is collected for advertising eligibility. Parents, guardians and schools should help learners protect their PIN and avoid sharing results with unintended recipients. 8. Policy changes

When data handling changes, the operator will update the published policy and its effective date and provide additional notices or choices where applicable. The published policy and Play disclosures must match the app version being distributed.
